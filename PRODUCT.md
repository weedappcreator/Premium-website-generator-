# Taconazo24

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Hungry people in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic — late-night eaters, families, delivery customers, and walk-ins. Anyone craving authentic Mexican street food at any hour.

## Product Purpose

A mouth-watering, 3D-scrolling restaurant website that makes you hungry instantly. Showcases Taconazo24's handmade tortillas (sin gluten), 24-hour service, and vibrant menu. The site IS the brand experience — bold Mexican colors, artisanal vibe, 24/7 energy.

## Brand Personality

Bold, warm, unapologetically Mexican. This is a taqueria that never sleeps — the energy of a late-night Santo Domingo street food scene meets handmade artisanal craft. Three-word personality: **vibrant, authentic, unstoppable**.

## Color Direction

Warm Mexican palette with punch. Think: deep earthy reds, vibrant oranges, cactus greens, warm yellows. Not generic "Mexican restaurant" clichés — refined with purpose.

## Typography Direction

A bold display font for headlines (something with Mexican character — not generic) paired with a clean, readable sans-serif for body text and menus.

## Design Principles

1. **Make them hungry.** Every visual should trigger appetite. Food photos, warm tones, mouth-watering layouts.
2. **24-hour energy.** The site should feel alive at any time — vibrant during the day, warm and glowing at night.
3. **Handmade pride.** The artisanal tortilla-making process is a feature, not an afterthought. Show the craft.
4. **Sin gluten as strength.** GF isn't a restriction here — it's the default and it's delicious.
5. **Mobile-first, Santo Domingo first.** Most customers are on phones, in motion, deciding where to eat right now.

## Anti-references

- Generic fast-food chains (McDonald's, etc.)
- Dark corporate restaurant sites with moody photography
- Over-designed fine dining minimalism — this is street food, keep it accessible
- Purple gradients and glassmorphism (AI default)

## Accessibility

- Minimum 4.5:1 contrast on all menu text (critical for readability on mobile in varying light)
- Touch targets ≥44px (menu items are tappable)
- prefers-reduced-motion respected
